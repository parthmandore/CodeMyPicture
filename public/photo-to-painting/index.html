How the Painting Engine Works

Unlike simple photo filter apps that slap a brush texture over pixels, CodeMyPicture algorithmically constructs a genuine digital painting from the ground up:

Stage 01

Planar Decomposition

478-point facial mesh mapping analyzes anatomical light and shadow planes across the forehead, cheeks, nose, and chin.

Stage 02

Soft Skin Filtering

Selective regional smoothing reduces sensor noise across cheek, forehead, and jaw landmarks while keeping feature boundaries intact.

Stage 03

Layered Brushwork

Empirical palette clustering synthesizes cohesive planar masses across hair, eyes, and skin tones with authentic portrait depth.

Stage 04

Underlay Fusion

A regional underlay blur foundation eliminates white vector seams, producing an organic digital oil/acrylic painting.

Key Artistic Features of Painting Mode

Designed specifically for expressive portraiture, Painting mode incorporates multiple specialized rendering passes:

Soft Skin Filtering

Calibrated Gaussian and bilateral filtering harmonizes skin tones across facial planes, giving skin a smooth, painted texture while retaining natural bone structure.

Expressive Eye & Contour Modeling

Targeted iris color clusters and high-resolution contour tracking preserve natural catchlights and gaze depth without artificial stamps.

Zero Seam Artifacts

Painting Mode vs. Exact Copy Mode

Choosing between Painting and Exact Copy depends entirely on your creative goal:

Painting Mode (Artistic Interpretation)

Prioritizes emotional aesthetic, painterly brushwork, and portrait harmony over rigid pixel accuracy.

  • Soft skin filtering and planar anatomical modeling
  • Directional hair brushwork and limbal eye gradients
  • Stylized oil/acrylic digital painting aesthetic
  • Best for gifts, social avatars, and wall art

Exact Copy Mode (Faithful Vector Tracing)

Prioritizes geometric fidelity, tight color quantization, and crisp posterized contour tracking.

  • High-resolution edge detection with minimal smoothing
  • Preserves exact photographic specular highlights
  • Graphic posterized vector look
  • Best for logos, technical diagrams, and crisp graphic avatars

Frequently Asked Questions

What makes Painting mode different from a standard vector trace?

Standard vector tracing creates flat, mechanical color polygons that often look posterized and rigid. Painting mode uses algorithmic planar facial modeling to decompose the face into anatomical planes, clusters organic color masses across hair and facial contours, and synthesizes natural iris depth.

How does the soft skin filter work in Painting mode?

The soft skin filter applies selective pre-trace smoothing calibrated across cheek, forehead, and chin facial landmarks. This softens camera noise and harsh digital pixelation while preserving sharp, crisp vector boundaries for the eyes, eyelashes, and lips.

Why are there no white gaps between painted vector paths?

CodeMyPicture synthesizes a regional underlay blur layer beneath the detail brushwork. This foundation matches the dominant local color tones, ensuring that anti-aliasing seams and sub-pixel rounding errors never produce white hairline gaps.

Can I scale the painted artwork to large print sizes?

Yes. Even though the aesthetic resembles an oil or acrylic digital painting, the output is pure mathematical vector geometry (SVG). It can be scaled to canvas prints, posters, or architectural displays with zero loss of resolution.

Can I inspect and customize the SVG painting in code?

Yes. The built-in Studio Workbench includes an interactive CodeMirror 6 code editor where you can view every SVG group (#underlay, #features, #contours), edit stroke colors, or export drop-in React JSX components.
